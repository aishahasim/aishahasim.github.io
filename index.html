Research

KFUPM BrainLab · 2026 – now

Graded "I don't know" in vision-language models

A linear probe on a VLM's hidden state gives a training-free grade of how damaged an image is. We use it to switch or steer models toward abstaining instead of guessing.

38→74%abstention on destroyed scans
31→13%wrong answers
7VLMs tested
KFUPM BrainLab · ongoing

Visual sycophancy

Designing evaluations that separate perception failures from capitulation: when a model abandons correct visual evidence just to agree with a user's pushback.

M.S. Graduate Project · 2026 – now

Regulation-to-code traceability

Graph-based retrieval that traces source code to GDPR requirements, with ablations isolating which component (data, retriever or model) causes hallucinations.
