About me

A little more about me

I grew up between two countries. I went to the British Grammar School in Dhahran, in classrooms with friends from dozens of nationalities, then finished school and my engineering degree in Karachi, and came back to Saudi Arabia for my master's at KFUPM. Moving between systems taught me to adapt fast, and that the same idea lands differently depending on who's listening.

I studied electrical engineering, and a machine learning course changed my direction. Leading my final-year project on vision-language models turned into a paper, a symposium talk and a Silver Medal. What stayed with me was that the models almost never signalled doubt, and that's what I've worked on since.

Outside work, I care about access. I founded The Circle of Sisterhood at KFUPM, taught English to children at FESWO in Korangi, Karachi, and, as Head of the Mental Health Society in college, ran a fundraiser for mental health care and an awareness event for students and parents.
